2 · Elements of Deep Learning

We train a network by minimising a loss function, chosen for regression or for classification, with gradient descent on mini-batches, and count the training in iterations and epochs. The lecture surveys the main architectures, discusses what makes a network well trained, and describes a trained network as a model of the probability distribution of its training data. It also reviews the factors behind the recent success of deep learning. The first complete example is the classification of MNIST digits as non-linear function fitting with the categorical cross-entropy.
